1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) operating on digital commerce platforms face systemic liquidity risk caused by the temporal mismatch between daily customer orders and platform settlement cycles (ranging from 1 to 14 days). Traditional financial underwriting models fail because individual SME transaction volumes exhibit high intermittency (39.53% zero-sales days in Latin American e-commerce).

FinSight AI solves this fundamental challenge by introducing the Dual-Resolution Inflow Theorem combined with a Neuro-Symbolic Tool-Grounded Copilot:

  • Micro-Daily Sales Forecasting: An Adaptive Trend-Calibrated CatBoost model with asymmetric quantile loss ($P_{10}, P_{50}, P_{90}$) delivering 59.80% WAPE, a +25.81% relative gain over naive persistence.
  • Macro-Treasury Inflow Aggregation: Transforming daily Poisson arrivals through a rolling convolution operator that acts as a low-pass filter, achieving an extraordinary 0.93% WAPE ($R^2 = 0.9994$) for working capital solvency audits.
  • Zero-Hallucination Copilot: LLM interactions are strictly grounded in deterministic Python/TypeScript integer-cent arithmetic tool contracts, guaranteeing zero mathematical hallucination during credit assessments.
