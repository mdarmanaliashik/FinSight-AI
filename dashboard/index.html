[NOTEBOOK 1 · EQ 1]

Syntetos-Boylan Demand Categorization

$$\text{ADI} = \frac{N}{\sum_{t=1}^N \mathbb{I}(d_t > 0)}, \qquad \text{CV}^2 = \left( \frac{\sigma_d}{\mu_d} \right)^2$$ $$\text{Smooth: } \text{ADI} < 1.32 \;\land\; \text{CV}^2 < 0.49 \quad\big|\quad \text{Intermittent: } \text{ADI} \ge 1.32 \;\land\; \text{CV}^2 < 0.49$$

Average Demand Interval ($\text{ADI}$) and squared coefficient of variation ($\text{CV}^2$) characterizing intermittency and lumpiness across transaction histories. Informs feature store architecture and justifies non-parametric tree ensembles over linear auto-regressors.
